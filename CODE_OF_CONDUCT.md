# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, explain technical disagreements with
evidence, and give others room to learn. Review the work without attacking its author. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, unwanted personal contact, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintenance contact listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
Request a private channel without including personal details in a public Issue. The maintainer
will consider the context and may request changes, moderate a discussion, or restrict participation.
If the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting tools or the relevant
institutional channel rather than relying on that person to investigate themselves.

These expectations apply to Issues, PRs, reviews, project discussions and communication made on
behalf of the project.
